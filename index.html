Case Study 01 • Healthcare

AI-Powered Proposal & RFP Drafting Automation

Turned multi-day RFP searches into a citation-grounded enterprise assistant.

The Challenge

A rapidly growing healthcare services organization was losing high-value contract bids due to slow proposal turnaround. Prior winning answers, case studies, and compliance methodologies were buried across disconnected shared drives and legacy documents.

The AARK Solution

AARK Innovations indexed the organization's entire proposal archive, clinical methodology decks, and credential databases into a secure enterprise vector lakehouse. We delivered an internal assistant that automatically generates first-draft answers, flags sections requiring human SME review, and provides source citations with one click.

Case Study 02 • Distribution

Streamlined Sales Quoting & CRM Automation

From 3-day spreadsheet bottlenecks down to 20-minute closed quotes.

The Challenge

Complex custom quoting was executed through fragile, unversioned Excel spreadsheets with tiered margin formulas trapped in key employees' heads, causing multi-day sales delays and pricing inconsistencies.

The AARK Solution

We eliminated the spreadsheets entirely by encoding complex product catalog rules directly into their CRM. We automated proposal PDF compilation, margin verification, and established automated management approval routing for out-of-band discounts.
